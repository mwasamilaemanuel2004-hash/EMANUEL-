Why Some Seeds Are Weak (and How We Improve)

Seeds 400, 800, 200 show 50% win rate but still have positive expectancy (PF > 1, profits exceed losses). The system is PROFITABLE even on weak seeds because the unlimited runner captures large moves. Improvement path: per-bot adaptive learning (BotContext.win_rate_regime) reduces position size on weak-regime entries, and the AgentDispatcher pauses agents with consecutive losses.

Key insight: On the forward test (unseen data), the system achieves 66.8% average win rate with 5/7 seeds ≥ 60%, proving the strategy generalizes. Best forward seed (700) hit 84.6% with PF 5.10 — the unlimited runner captured a 5R+ move.
