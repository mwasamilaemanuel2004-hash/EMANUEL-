Analysis Framework — 10 Weighted Factors

1. Regulatory Clarity

2. Tokenization & RWA Growth

Weight: 15%. RWA TVL (real-world assets on-chain) and 30-day growth rate. Higher TVL + positive growth = higher score. Captures the institutional adoption of tokenized treasuries, bonds, and real estate.

3. Product Launches

Weight: 10%. Recent protocol/token launches signal ecosystem momentum. Each launch adds 20 points (capped at 100).

4. Ecosystem Adoption

Weight: 12%. Active users, developer activity, integrations. Captures real usage vs. speculation.

5. Macro Liquidity

Weight: 10%. Fed policy, global M2, risk-on/off cycles. High macro liquidity = bullish for crypto.

6. Network Upgrades

Weight: 8%. Protocol upgrades (Ethereum, Solana, L2s). Catalysts like EIPs, restaking, or new VMs drive momentum.

7. Staking & ETFs

Weight: 8%. Institutional product availability (spot ETFs, staking products). Binary: 100 if available, 50 otherwise.

8. Capital Flow

Weight: 12%. 30-day net on-chain flow (+ = inflow). Positive flow = accumulation = bullish.

9. Market Cycle / Timing

Weight: 10%. Cycle phase: BULL_RUN UPGRADE_CATALYST ACCUMULATION BEAR
Plus timing quality: cycle + flow + upgrade catalyst.

10. Real Usage

Weight: 15%. Active addresses, transaction volume, fees burned. Measures actual on-chain utility vs. speculative trading.
